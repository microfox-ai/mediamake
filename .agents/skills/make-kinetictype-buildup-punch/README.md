# Make kinetictype buildup punch

A style of [[make-kinetictype]]. The quote arrives one giant word at a time, each slammed onto a hit of
the music: a build on the riser, then the one word that matters, letter by letter on the drop. The
picture is restyled in several art styles and flashes between them, its characters cut out so they stand
in front of the type. The end rushes back into the start, so the Short loops.

The style is how the words move. The fonts, the colours and the art styles are chosen for each video,
from its own picture, quote and track.

## How to use

1. Copy the install prompt and paste it into Claude. Install Browse Hack with it: it runs Midjourney,
   the research and the upload.
2. Give Claude a picture, a quote and an instrumental, and say: **make a buildup punch short of this
   quote**. Tell it which word is the hero word, the one to shout.
3. Pick the stretch of the track by ear from the three previews it cuts: a quiet build into a drop.
4. Say how big a Midjourney round it may run, then pick the restyled pictures you like.
5. Open the review link and leave notes on the video. Claude fixes them and shows 10-second previews.
6. Say when it is right. Claude renders the master, delivers it and fills the YouTube upload form. You
   press Publish.

## What it uses

- [[make-kinetictype]]: the family's shared steps, which this style follows: the music, the research,
  the pictures, the checks, the sound, the delivery.
- [[revise-video]]: the review loop for the video.
- [[revise-image]]: where you pick the Midjourney restyles.
- [[mcp/browse-hack]] (required): Midjourney restyles of the picture and its close-ups, the type
  research on Pinterest, the YouTube upload. Without it there is no restyled art: the edit runs on the
  one picture, and you upload by hand.
- [[mcp/elevenlabs]] (optional): sound effects the media library doesn't have. It shows the price first.
- [[mcp/fal]] (optional): a picture or an upscale when Midjourney isn't available. It shows the price
  first.

## What the skill outputs

A looping 9:16 Short (1080 × 1920, about 24 seconds: as long as the stretch of music) in your
workspace's `out/<slug>/`, with a sound effect on every hit mixed over the music. With it come the
restyled plates and cut-outs, the edit as one data file (`video/edit.json`) and, once you approve it,
the master in your channel's folder with its `youtube/` pack.
