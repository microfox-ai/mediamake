# Podcast, interview

Channels read: @TheDiaryOfACEO, @lexfridman, @hubermanlab, @ChrisWillx, @ColinandSamir
(`mm thumbs inspire podcast-interview @TheDiaryOfACEO @lexfridman @hubermanlab @ChrisWillx @ColinandSamir`).

## What the hits share

- **The guest's face** is on 39 of 40. Who the guest is decides most: the same frame wins with a
  famous name and loses with an unknown one.
- **The most words of any kind**: median 5, up to 10, and only 6 have none. The line is a claim,
  a topic or a name, set large (28 of 40), not giant.
- **Sentence or title case** as often as caps (16 title case, 10 caps): it reads as something a
  person said.
- **A plate or one coloured word does the work** (15); strokes, glows and shadows are almost absent.
- **Black grounds** (27 of 40) and a **fixed series frame**: a logo on 17, an episode number on 7.

## Archetypes for the board

1. **Claim between two faces** (The Diary Of A CEO): the guest left, the host right with a hand at
   the chin, a 4–8 word claim in the middle with the key words on a red plate, the show's badge.
2. **Name plate + portrait** (Lex Fridman): black, one side-lit face, the guest's name stacked in
   white, the episode number in green, the show's name in blue. Nothing else.
3. **Topic between two portraits** (Huberman Lab): black-and-white faces either side, the topic in
   white caps (3–5 words), the guest's name small, a logo box.
4. **Highlighted name or quote** (Colin and Samir): a big face, white words with the key ones on
   yellow plates, a small handwritten kicker ("The full story of"), or a quote in quotation marks.
5. **Framed inset** (Chris Williamson): two faces flank one red-framed picture that stands for the
   topic; no words.
6. **Title card**: tall condensed caps with one word in the accent, the guest's name small below.
7. **One strange portrait, no words**: a prop or a stunt on the guest's face that poses the topic.
8. **Versus**: two guests facing each other with their names and "vs" for a debate.

## Type

A heavy grotesque in title case for claims; a tall condensed sans or a DIN-style face for names
and numbers; a small handwritten or serif kicker. Fonts: SKILL.md, "podcast, interview".

## Colour and ground

Near-monochrome and one accent a show: red (DOAC), yellow (Colin and Samir), green and blue (Lex
Fridman), a blue frame (Huberman Lab), teal (Chris Williamson). Faces are lit from the side
against black, or cut out onto it.

## Title + thumbnail

Half complement, half repeat. A claim on the thumbnail lets the title carry who said it ("Epstein
Was A Honey Trap" + the guest's role in the title). A name on the thumbnail lets the title carry
the topic.

## Hits vs misses

The guest. Also: the short "essentials" cut loses to the full episode in the same family of frame;
a tweet screenshot or a third element makes the claim layout too busy; a topic with no person
loses to a person.

## Build notes

- Two portraits must match: same crop height, same grade (`grade: mono` for both, or both colour),
  eyes on one line.
- A highlight plate is its own `text` layer for the key words, so the plate hugs them.
- The frame (badge, number style, accent, which side the host sits) is fixed for the show:
  SKILL.md, "Series". Each episode changes the guest, the line and the highlighted words.
- A quote in quotation marks must be the guest's real words from the transcript.
