# Gaming

Channels read: @jacksepticeye, @markiplier, @Grian, @videogamedunkey, @GameTheory
(`mm thumbs inspire gaming @jacksepticeye @markiplier @Grian @videogamedunkey @GameTheory`).

## What the hits share

- **The game's own character or art is the hero** (29 of 40). A human face is on only 7.
- **The game's logo** is on 20 of 40: for a new or famous game it is the most-read thing there.
- **Mostly wordless**: 22 of 40 have no added words (median 0, never more than 5). Added words
  carry a black stroke (18) and a shadow (11).
- **A scene from the game as the ground** (17), saturated; dark only for horror.
- **Series marks**: the channel's logo (8) or an episode number (5).

## Archetypes for the board

1. **Key art + the game's logo** (videogamedunkey): official art or a striking captured frame, the
   logo large, nothing else.
2. **One character fills the frame**: a close-up of the monster, the hero, the strange face;
   no words (Markiplier, jacksepticeye).
3. **The creator inside the game**: their face or avatar composited into the world (a diving mask
   over the game's logo; a cashier's apron), smiling or screaming.
4. **Avatar + series logo + episode number** (Grian): the outlined skin in the centre, the season's
   logo top, EPISODE 01 as a block bottom left. Openers win.
5. **Theory call-out** (Game Theory): saturated mascot art, 2–3 tilted words in green and white
   with a heavy stroke ("All The LORE!"), an arrow or a prop, the channel's badge.
6. **A crowd facing something huge**: small characters, backs to the camera, question and
   exclamation marks over their heads.
7. **Split with a verdict**: two panels with a ✓ and a ✕, or two characters face to face.
8. **A tiny joke caption**: one small lower-case word and an arrow on official art ("you →").

## Type

Rounded or heavy sans with a thick stroke, caps or mixed case, tilted; green or yellow on dark is
the classic. Pixel type for retro and block games. Fonts: SKILL.md, "gaming".

## Colour and ground

Whatever the game's palette is, pushed: the thumbnail should look like the game at its best.
Characters separate by a white outline, blur behind them, or sheer size.

## Title + thumbnail

Most have no words and let the title speak (22). Series repeat their name and number (10). A hook
word that adds something is the minority and works ("My Biggest Acting Role Yet" + THAT'S ME →).

## Hits vs misses

The game and the moment decide: a season opener, a famous franchise, part 1. Visible misses: dark
moody frames with no face and no hook, a calm face, a bare vista, part 2s, off-topic videos.

## Build notes

- Capture at the game's highest quality and pick the sharpest frame; UI and HUD cropped out unless
  the HUD is the joke.
- The game's logo comes from its press kit, placed whole; never redraw or recolour it.
- An episode block is two `text` layers (EPISODE small, the number giant) in one corner, the same
  place every episode (SKILL.md, "Series").
