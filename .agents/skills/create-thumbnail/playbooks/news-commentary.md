# News, commentary

Channels read: @LastWeekTonight, @penguinz0, @PhilipDeFranco, @ColdFusion, @TLDRnewsGlobal
(`mm thumbs inspire news-commentary @LastWeekTonight @penguinz0 @PhilipDeFranco @ColdFusion @TLDRnewsGlobal`).
Read from the sheet only (not tagged one by one), plus the user's own Doomcat episodes in the
feedback log.

## What the hits share

- **The person or thing in the news is the picture**: a leader, a company's chief, the footage
  itself. The host is there as the show's face, or not at all.
- **A verdict or a question in 2–4 words**, never the headline in full: WHOOPS, IT BACKFIRED!!,
  A BLUE TSUNAMI?, `$122 BILLION WIPED`.
- **The strongest frames of any kind**: each of the five shows could be named from a blurred
  thumbnail. The frame is the brand; the story changes inside it.

## Archetypes for the board

1. **The show's own frame** (Last Week Tonight): the host at the desk with the over-the-shoulder
   graphic naming the story. For a show with a set, a clean frame of it is the thumbnail.
2. **Host's reaction + the people involved** (Philip DeFranco): the host's face on the left, a
   bright-framed collage of who the story is about, a 2–3 word reaction in white and yellow, a
   circle or arrow on the detail.
3. **News photo + a headline** (ColdFusion): one darkened press photo of the person, the logo top
   left, a 2–4 word line in a wide sans with the number in it, or one dry word (Whoops).
4. **Explainer card** (TLDR News): the brand's colour field, a cutout of the figure, a map or a
   chart, a 3–4 word question on plates.
5. **The raw frame** (penguinz0): an undesigned screenshot of the thing being discussed. It works
   for a channel whose promise is "look at this"; it is a choice, not a default.
6. **The number is the story**: one figure as large as the face it belongs to.
7. **The host, big, and the story's subject as the hook** (the user's Doomcat rules): the host in
   every thumbnail and recognisable, the subject cut out large where the eye lands, the headline
   filling the top third in two width-fit lines.

## Type

A wide or heavy sans for headlines, with one accent word; plates for questions. The show's own
display face for a comedy show. Fonts: SKILL.md, "news, finance".

## Colour and ground

The show's colour, always: purple (TLDR), red and yellow (DeFranco), the blue set (Last Week
Tonight), near-black with white (ColdFusion). Dark versions beat pale ones at feed size (the
user's Doomcat finding).

## Title + thumbnail

The thumbnail reacts or asks; the title reports. A figure or a claim on the thumbnail must be in
the video and true: news is where a misleading thumbnail does most harm.

## Hits vs misses

The story decides. Visible misses: off-format episodes (a web extra, a game stream on a commentary
channel), a layout with a third or fourth face added.

## Build notes

- Real people are the subject: press or the user's own pictures, cut out cleanly, never a
  generated likeness, and nothing that puts words in their mouth.
- The frame goes in the brand's `thumbnail.json` (SKILL.md, "Series"); each episode still gets a
  layout made for its own story inside that frame.
