# Travel, vlog

Channels read: @yestheory, @KaraandNate, @drewbinsky, @baldandbankrupt, @IndigoTraveller
(`mm thumbs inspire travel-vlog @yestheory @KaraandNate @drewbinsky @baldandbankrupt @IndigoTraveller`).

## What the hits share

- **A real photograph of the place** (38 of 40), in daylight, saturated. No studio, no flat field.
- **The traveller as witness**: a face is on 36 of 40 and leads 24, looking at the camera worried
  or wide-eyed (serious on 18, surprised on 6), or looking at the place.
- **One name, one price or one claim, or nothing**: 19 have no words; median 1, never more than 4.
  Words are on 21 of 40 hits and on only 3 of 15 misses: a wordless selfie with no named place is
  the commonest miss.
- The channel's logo sits bottom left on 8.

## The kit (gather before the board)

- the trip's best frame of the place
- the traveller cut out from a frame with matching light
- the country's flag (Commons); a price or a map pin

## Archetypes for the board

1. **Place name + witness + the scene** (Indigo Traveller): the country in caps across the top,
   the host on the right looking into a dense street behind. One layout, every episode.
2. **Face in the scene, no words** (Drew Binsky): a wide-angle selfie in the middle of something
   strange, a flag for where.
3. **A claim over the place** (Yes Theory): THIS IS NOT PARIS across a wide shot; the place itself
   is the surprise.
4. **The stay is the hero** (Kara and Nate): a villa, a train cabin, a truck, people tiny for
   scale, and a price (`$8,000 PER NIGHT`).
5. **Cosy portrait**: a person at ease inside the cabin or on the terrace, the view through the
   window; no words.
6. **Mystery pointer**: an arrow, a circle or a magnified inset on one spot of a landscape
   (LAWLESS CITY →; a house in a jungle, circled).
7. **Raw frame + a stamp**: an unpolished shot with one giant condensed word or a stamp over it
   (AFGHANISTAN, DANGER). Honest, and it suits channels built on being there.
8. **Two numbers**: "19 HOURS" against "2 MINS" on two held phones, the vehicles behind.

## Type

Heavy sans caps, white or black, top-aligned, with a light stroke or shadow so it holds over sky
and street; a brush plate under a claim. Handwriting suits a personal vlog's small line. Fonts:
SKILL.md, "tall, punchy" and "handwritten, personal".

## Colour and ground

Blue sky, turquoise water, green hills: nature's own saturation, pushed a little. Red or yellow
for arrows and stamps. Dark frames are rare and read worst (a dim train corridor).

## Title + thumbnail

Place names repeat the title's place (14 repeat) and that is fine: the country is the search term.
A price adds the proof the title leaves out ("Most Expensive Overwater Villa" + the nightly rate).

## Hits vs misses

Mostly where they went: danger, extremes and luxury win. Visible misses: personal updates (a
subscriber count, a tattoo, a van bed), a close-up with no place, a quiet selfie with no word.

## Build notes

- Use the trip's own best frame: sharp, level, the place recognisable. Brighten the place, keep
  the sky.
- The witness is a cutout from another frame only when the light matches; a mismatched cutout on
  a landscape reads as fake at once.
- Pure generated scenery is wrong for a real trip: the thumbnail must show where they went.
