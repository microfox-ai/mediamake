# Fitness, self-improvement

Channels read: @JeffNippard, @athleanx, @WillTennyson, @JesseJamesWest, @mattdavella
(`mm thumbs inspire fitness-selfimprovement @JeffNippard @athleanx @WillTennyson @JesseJamesWest @mattdavella`).

## What the hits share

- **The body is the hero** (a figure on 15 of 40, a face on 10), shown doing or showing the thing.
- **Annotated**: a split or before / after (10), arrows (10), a versus (6), a tier list, a muscle
  tinted red, a number on the body (25 % / 8 %).
- **Words are an order or a verdict**: median 2, and 18 have none. Caps (19), with the key word on
  a red plate (11) or in the accent (7). A few calm ones use lower case (4).
- **Dark gym grounds** (16) or bright natural scenes (14).

## Archetypes for the board

1. **Giant order + the trainer** (Athlean-X): FIX THIS, PLEASE STOP, DO THESE EXERCISES FOREVER in
   white caps with one word on a red plate, the exercise shown, an arrow at the joint or muscle.
2. **Tier list** (Jeff Nippard): an S-to-F colour column on the left, a posed lifter on the right,
   the muscle in question tinted.
3. **Before / after or two numbers**: two bodies or two days side by side with a figure on each
   (25 % / 8 %; DAY 1 / DAY 100).
4. **Ladder of levels**: NOOB → NOVICE → AVERAGE → ELITE → PRO stacked, an arrow at one.
5. **Group against group** (Jesse James West): two teams facing each other across the frame, no
   words; the contrast is the hook.
6. **Host between two worlds** (Will Tennyson): the host in the middle of two opposite scenes
   (two countries, two diets), a small lower-case reaction ("it's depressing.").
7. **Number on a feat**: `800 LBS →` on the bar, `#3` with an arrow at the muscle.
8. **Quiet object from above** (Matt D'Avella): hands and one object on a clean desk in warm
   light, no words or three in lower case. The self-improvement half of this kind.

## Type

Tall condensed or heavy sans caps for orders; a thin lower-case sans for the calm half. Fonts:
SKILL.md, "sport, fitness" and "calm, minimal".

## Colour and ground

Black, white and red in the gym; saturated daylight for challenges; warm wood and white for calm.
The body separates by a rim of light on a dark gym, or by a clean cutout on white.

## Title + thumbnail

The picture warns or proves; the title promises: "Most EFFECTIVE Way to Get Abs" + PLEASE STOP;
"Smartest Way To Get Lean" + BEFORE / AFTER. 17 complement, 2 repeat.

## Hits vs misses

Misses are Q&As, training-week diaries and plain gym poses with no question. A thumbnail with no
problem to fix and no result to show has nothing to click.

## Build notes

- A tier list, a ladder and a tinted muscle are drawn layers: `shape` rects with `text`, and a
  red `fill` clipped to a hand-made mask (a job `custom/` component when the shape is organic).
- Before / after pairs need the same pose, crop and light on both sides, with a hard split.
- Health claims and numbers must be the video's own: no invented results on a body.
