# Software, coding and AI tutorials

Channels read: @Fireship, @NetworkChuck, @KevinStratvert, @WebDevSimplified, @JeffSu
(`mm thumbs inspire software-tutorial @Fireship @NetworkChuck @KevinStratvert @WebDevSimplified @JeffSu`).

## What the hits share

- **The words are the picture** more than in any other kind: type is the hero on 17 of 40, and 23
  set their biggest line giant. Only 6 have no words. Median 2 words, never more than 5.
- **The tool is named or shown**: its logo on 27 of 40, a slice of its real screen on 18. A viewer
  looking for "Claude", "Excel" or "MCP" must find that word or mark in a glance.
- **A friendly face** is on 36 of 40 (smiling on 20), usually to one side, often pointing at the
  words or the screen. It is the hero on only 11.
- **Dark or composite grounds** (27 of 40); white is rare.
- **A fixed frame** on 16 of 40 (a logo or the same layout every time).

## The kit (gather before the board)

- the tool's **real screen at the moment of the result**, cropped to the one control or output (`thumbs shot`, or the user's screenshot of their own app)
- the **official logo of the tool** and of every tool or destination in the story (`thumbs logo`): shown as a logo card or app tiles
- **a figure that presents it**: the host cut out pointing; with no host, a character or object from the result, cut out
- **the result itself**, where it lives (the output file, the page it went live on)

## Archetypes for the board

1. **Stacked claim + a borrowed face** (Fireship): a 2–3 line word block on the left in cream and
   one accent colour, a meme or famous face on the right, dark ground, the series badge in a corner.
2. **Tool name + pointing host** (Kevin Stratvert): the tool's name huge on a coloured plate, the
   word TUTORIAL or the time it takes ("10 MIN"), the logo, a card of the real UI, the host pointing.
3. **Reaction face + one glowing object, no words** (NetworkChuck): the title does all the telling.
4. **Code or diagram board** (Web Dev Simplified): one highlighted word or phrase on a brush plate
   over a real code block or diagram, the face low on the right.
5. **Host with the app icons around them** (Jeff Su): logos orbit the face, one caps line below
   ("AI AGENTS").
6. **Before / after or slop / clean split**: two panels with a label each and an arrow between.
7. **Steps**: "Step 1 → Step 2" drawn across the picture with the tools' icons as the stops.
8. **Verdict mark**: a red ✕ or green ✓ on a piece of code or a habit ("Never Again", "AVOID THIS").

## Type

Heavy sans caps for the claim, with a black stroke and shadow (16 and 10 of 40) or on a plate (9);
one word in the accent colour (7). Title case reads fine too (10). A second small voice helps:
pixel or mono type for a sub line (Fireship), handwriting for a step. Fonts: SKILL.md, "clean,
tech", "code" and "tall, punchy".

## Colour and ground

One accent a channel: red (Fireship), cyan (Web Dev Simplified), orange and yellow (Kevin
Stratvert), purple (NetworkChuck). The host is cut out with a glow or an outline over a dark or
gradient ground; a blurred office is the bright alternative.

## Title + thumbnail

Searchable tutorials repeat the tool's name on the thumbnail and that is right (9 repeats):
"How to Use Claude AI" over HOW TO USE CLAUDE AI. News-style ones complement (25): the title gives
the topic, the thumbnail the verdict or the joke ("Never Again", "CLAUDE CRACKED").

## Hits vs misses

The tool decides: a hot consumer tool beats a niche one in the same layout. Visible misses: no title
word at all, seven small words, a blurred screen as the picture, the channel's podcast or event
videos.

## A series with no face (Cook with Claude, 2026-11-15)

When the channel shows no host, the **output is the hero** (one result, at least half the frame's
height) and the words are **its plain name, the term a viewer would search for** (ALBUM COVER.),
not a pun or a step of the process: puns read as decoration beside the hits' tool names. The
series' frame (tag, logo, ground, one accent word) and its signature object stay.

## Build notes

- Fetch the tool's official logo (SKILL.md, "Logos") and keep it whole and unrecoloured.
- A UI slice is an `image` layer of a real screenshot with `radius` and `shadow`, tilted a few
  degrees at most; crop to the one control the video is about, never a whole window.
- A series (a course, a weekly show) sets its frame once (SKILL.md, "Series") and changes only the
  words, the face or object, and the accent word.
