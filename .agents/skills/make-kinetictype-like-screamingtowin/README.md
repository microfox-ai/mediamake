# Make kinetictype like Screaming to Win

The [[make-kinetictype-buildup-punch]] style, pinned to one example: Screaming to Win, a Blue Lock
Short. It keeps that video's look (heavy condensed caps in ink, paper and one accent colour, the picture
restyled in print and paint looks, music with sound effects and no voice) and designs everything else
from what you give it: the length, the sections and which word does what come from your quote, your
picture and your track, never from the example.

You need only one of the three. Claude makes the others, shows them and goes on once you are happy.

## How to use

1. Copy the install prompt and paste it into Claude. Install Browse Hack with it.
2. Give Claude a picture, a quote or a song and say: **make one like Screaming to Win**.
3. Answer its one round of questions: which word is the hero word, how big a Midjourney round it may
   run, the channel, and how to make what is missing (a picture, three quote options, a track from your
   library or Suno).
4. Pick the stretch of the track by ear from the three previews.
5. The next thing you see is the first preview, at the review link. Leave notes on the video; Claude
   fixes them.
6. Say when it is right. Claude renders the master, delivers it and fills the YouTube upload form. You
   press Publish.

## What it uses

- [[make-kinetictype-buildup-punch]]: the style. Its rules, its storyboard and its sound cues.
- [[make-kinetictype]]: the family's shared steps, which the style follows.
- [[revise-video]]: the review loop for the video.
- [[mcp/browse-hack]] (required): Midjourney for the restyles and for a picture when you have none,
  Pinterest for the research, Suno for a track when you have none, and the YouTube upload. Without it,
  you bring all three inputs and upload by hand.
- [[mcp/elevenlabs]] (optional): sound effects the media library doesn't have. It shows the price first.
- [[mcp/fal]] (optional): a picture when Midjourney isn't available. It shows the price first.

## What the skill outputs

A looping 9:16 Short (1080 × 1920, 20 to 35 seconds: as long as the music's own arc) in your
workspace's `out/<slug>/`, and after your sign-off the master in your channel's folder with its
`youtube/` pack. Whatever it made on the way is kept too: the picture, the quote options, the track.
