MediaMake Spaces reads and fills the Cloud Library. `list_library` shows the spaces you can open, their collections, and one collection's fields, file slots and guide. `search_library` answers with cards, `get_library_entry` and `read_library_entry` give fields and text with no download, and `fetch_library_files` brings files into a folder, each checked against its SHA-256. `library_made_from` says what was already made from an exact file (its transcript, its stems) before it is paid for again. `save_library_entry` saves nothing until the person agreed to the sentence it gives, and stops on private details found in a file.
