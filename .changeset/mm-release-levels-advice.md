mm-release speaks the store's new levels and advice. `catalog_set_readiness` sets an item's level: alpha, beta or production (ready is the same), and alpha and beta keep its proof for later. `catalog_set_listing` also sets editorsPick and advice (recommended, not-recommended or deprecated, with adviceNote and insteadOf). `catalog_items` filters by level, alpha included.
