MediaMake Spaces reads and works a project's to-do queue. `list_todos`, `post_todo`, `claim_todo` and `finish_todo` post a step one place cannot do (an upload that needs a signed-in browser) for a place that can, pick it up once and record what came of it. `where_it_runs` reads the project's register: which cloud environment and which opened computer can do what.
